What is included. Every sit down restaurant, quick service spot, snack stand, coffee kiosk, lounge and pool bar I could find at the four theme parks and the Disney resort hotels, plus the Swan, Dolphin and Swan Reserve. I compared the list against Disney Food Blog, WDWInfo, the Disney Dining Plan lists and Disney Tourist Blog's open and closed report. Small carts are grouped into one "snack carts" card per park. Disney Springs and the water parks are left out as you asked, along with ESPN Wide World of Sports, Shades of Green and the Four Seasons.

Left off because they are closed or not operating: Tomorrowland Terrace and Tortuga Tavern (Magic Kingdom, seasonal and not running recently), Restaurant Marrakesh (EPCOT, closed long term), and the permanently closed Mama Melrose's, PizzeRizzo, Restaurantosaurus, Aunt Polly's, ESPN Club, Jellyrolls and Spirit of Aloha.

Where the ratings come from. TripAdvisor bubble ratings (out of 5, with review counts), AllEars reader scores, TouringPlans reader surveys and the 2026 Michelin Guide appear on the cards. "Picked by" counts how many of 14 recent best-of lists named the place: Disney Food Blog, Theme Park Shark, Chart the Magic, Practically Perfect Pixie Dust, Disney Tourist Blog (two lists), Mouse Hacking, The Points Guy, Fodor's, Ziggy Knows Disney, DisneyBlog, Eat Sleep Disney, Mickey Visit's Yelp roundup and TouringPlans readers. Many small stands have no rating because the sites did not cover them.

Price signs are rough guides ($ under about $15 a person, $$$$ is a splurge). Menus, hours and what is open change often, and a few places are brand new or still to open, so confirm details and reservations on Disney's site or in My Disney Experience before we book.
